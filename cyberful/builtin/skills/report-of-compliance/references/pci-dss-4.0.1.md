# PCI DSS 4.0.1 reporting profile

PCI SSC calls the formal deliverable a **Report on Compliance (ROC)**. The current profile is pinned to PCI DSS 4.0.1 and organizes report-ready evidence across scope, assessment method, Requirements 1–12, compensating or customized approaches, third parties, testing, and attestation readiness.

The generated document is not an official ROC, AOC, SAQ, certificate, or validation. PCI SSC recognizes its current official forms for documenting PCI DSS compliance; obtain the current ROC template and related instructions from the [PCI SSC Document Library](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/). Do not bundle, reconstruct, delete mandatory sections from, or imply approval of that template. Transfer only reviewed content into a legitimately obtained current template.

Record merchant or service-provider type, reporting scope, CDE boundaries, connected and security-impacting systems, segmentation reliance, third-party responsibilities, sampling, assessor organization, assessor qualification, testing dates, and evidence cutoff. Reconcile the profile with `trace-cardholder-data-environment`, `assess-pci-dss-readiness`, and `audit-pci-dss-penetration-test-evidence` when those procedures produced the source evidence.

Never infer `supported` from a policy title, screenshot, tool exit code, previous-year ROC, certificate, or vendor statement alone. Preserve not-tested requirements, remediation still in progress, expired evidence, sampling limitations, contradictory work papers, and not-applicable rationales for qualified review.

Official source: [PCI DSS 4.0.1](https://docs-prv.pcisecuritystandards.org/PCI%20DSS/Standard/PCI-DSS-v4_0_1.pdf). PCI SSC announced the corresponding ROC template on 28 August 2024 and directs users to the official document library.
