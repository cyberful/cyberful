# AI Risk Evidence Method

## Evidence grades

- `observed`: reproduced behavior or directly inspected implementation.
- `supported`: multiple consistent artifacts support the claim.
- `declared`: documentation or an owner states the claim without independent validation.
- `unknown`: decisive evidence is unavailable.

## Risk record

Tie each risk to an affected actor, unacceptable outcome, initiating condition, capability path, current controls, evidence grade, likelihood conditions, consequence boundaries, owner, treatment, and validation trigger. Keep safety, security, privacy, reliability, and governance effects separate when they require different owners.
